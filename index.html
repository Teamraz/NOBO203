The Trailer

Built for comfortable camping without giving up adventure.

We are selling our 2023 Forest River No Boundaries 20.3 travel trailer. Why? The wife just retired, and it's time for us to move to a slightly larger trailer (fifth wheel) as we extend our time on the road. Our NoBo has been a GREAT travel companion, and was the right size for us when we jumped into the RV world. We pulled it with our Ford F-150 3.5 Eco-boost with no problems. The NoBo 20.3 combines a very comfortable interior with the rugged, outdoor-oriented design that makes the line so popular.

The trailer offers an open living space, full kitchen, private bathroom, comfortable sleeping accommodations, generous storage, and a layout that works equally well for weekend camping or longer road trips. It has been carefully maintained and is ready to camp. We are happy to answer questions or arrange a time for serious buyers to see it in person.

Customizations

In addition to the great condition, we implemented several upgrades and customizations to facilitate life on the road. These include:

  • Office Space - The rear bunkhose was removed and an office was installed to allow us to work from the road. As an IT professional, I was able to write software and run Zoom meetings while off-grid (via cellular and Starlink). This unit would be FANTASTIC for an individual or a couple that wants to work from the road, or maintains a podcast.
  • Additional shelf storage next to the office - creating the office allowed us to add additional shelving next to the refrigerator. These shelves have been great for things like kitchen tools and office supplies.
  • Conversion of the dinette to a flip up table - As you can see in the pictures, we removed the stanchions supporting the factory tabletop and replaced it with a flip-up butcher block top. This has provided easier access to the dinette area and opened up access to the seats. If the additional sleeping space is needed in the dinette, the original top is included in the sale and that simply rests on top of the dinette seats for additional sleeping space.
  • Upgrade of the dinette seats to hinged access to the storage - NoBo kinda missed this in the design. The factory design is very poor for access to storage under the dinette seats. If you use only the door at the end of the seats, you have to pull everything out to get to the back. We removed the wooden seats, and reattached them using piano hinges. Now access is simple and fast!
  • Migration of the battery and upgrade to LIFEPO4 - We repositioned the battery cables to terminate under the murphy bed, and just installed a new 100 Amp-Hour LIFEPO4 battery. The trailer is currently wired to support TWO LIFEPO4 batteries. This trailer is wired to run off-grid for days supporting the AC outlets, office, TV, etc. The only appliance that does not run off the battery/inverter system is the Air Conditioner (due to the way Forest River designed it). If that is needed, a generator or DC-based AC unit would work fine.
  • Tires in great shape - The tires are like new with lots of tread left. They are designed to handle off-road environments.
